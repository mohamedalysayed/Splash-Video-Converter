> A one-pager for video creators, influencers, and ad partners making short-form content about Cast. Read this, then make videos people actually want to watch.

If you have a question this brief doesn't answer, email **muhammmedaly@gmail.com**.

## 1. The product in one sentence

**Cast is a beautiful video converter for macOS, Windows, and Linux. Buy once for $19, convert forever, no subscription.**

That's it. The whole pitch fits on a sticker. Don't over-explain it in your videos.

---

## 2. Who Cast is for

The viewer who should feel "this is for me" by the end of your video:

- **YouTubers** who shoot in MOV/MKV and need MP4 for uploads
- **Podcasters** converting WAV masters to MP3 for distribution
- **Editors** who hand off proxies and finals in different formats every day
- **Indie filmmakers** with folders of GoPro / drone clips
- **Devs and creators** who hate subscriptions and want desktop software they own
- **Anyone** who's ever opened HandBrake, scrolled past 14 settings tabs, and just wanted to convert a file

Avoid framing Cast as a "pro broadcast" tool. It's not for big TV studios with Avid workflows. It's for the 95% in the middle who just need files converted, fast, with no fuss.

## 3. Pain points to lean into

Lead with the pain. Make the viewer feel it in the first 3 seconds.

| Pain | What to show |
|---|---|
| **"My video is in the wrong format for Instagram"** | Phone showing the "this format isn't supported" error |
| **"HandBrake confuses me"** | Quick close-up of the HandBrake settings tab tree — 3 seconds of "ugh" face |
| **"Adobe wants $23/month forever"** | Adobe pricing page on screen + visible price tag |
| **"My MacBook fan is screaming"** | MacBook overheating, exporting one file at a time, taking forever |
| **"I have 40 files to convert and it's Friday night"** | Folder with dozens of clips, conversion taking forever |

## 4. The "wow moment" to capture

Every Cast ad needs at least one of these on camera:

1. **The drop** — drag a folder of 20+ video files onto Cast. They all populate the queue instantly.
2. **The parallel convert** — watch 4+ progress bars move at the same time on a multi-core machine.
3. **The drag-and-drop output** — finished file appearing right next to the source in Finder/Explorer.
4. **The cancel** — hit cancel mid-conversion, the partial file disappears.
5. **The icy drop zone hover** — the cursor hovering the drop zone, the shimmer passing across.

The drop is the single best shot. If you only film one thing, film that.

## 5. Hooks (the first 3 seconds)

Short-form content lives or dies in the first 3 seconds. Use these.

### Hook style 1 — the pain confessional
> *"I just spent two hours converting videos in HandBrake. Two hours. For something that should take five minutes."*

### Hook style 2 — the price stunt
> *"Adobe wants $276 a year for a video converter. I just paid $19, once, forever. Let me show you."*

### Hook style 3 — the visual punch
> *(No words. Pure visual: drop a folder of 30 files onto Cast, watch the queue light up, hit Start. The whole thing converts in seconds. Then cut to: "$19. Once.")*

### Hook style 4 — the comparison
> *"This is HandBrake. This is Cast. Same job. Tell me which one you'd rather use."* (split-screen)

### Hook style 5 — the disbelief
> *"I refuse to believe a video converter could be this pretty."* (cut to Cast UI on a giant monitor)

## 6. The script template

A 30-second UGC ad follows this rhythm:

```
[0-3s]   HOOK — pain, price, or visual punch
[3-8s]   PROBLEM — show the old way (HandBrake / Adobe / waiting)
[8-15s]  SOLUTION — drag a file onto Cast, hit Start
[15-22s] PROOF — show parallel queue / multi-format support / done in seconds
[22-27s] PRICE — "$19. Once. Lifetime updates."
[27-30s] CTA — "Link in bio. Cast by Splash."
```

For a 60-second ad, double the proof section. For a 15-second ad, cut the problem section and let the visual do the work.

## 7. The "do not" list

Things that will make your video feel like an ad (and tank engagement):

- ❌ **Don't say "revolutionary," "game-changing," "powerful," "robust."** Generic-marketing language. Real people don't talk like that.
- ❌ **Don't do a "5 features of Cast" listicle.** Boring. Pick one feature, show it cinematically.
- ❌ **Don't read a script in a flat voice.** Speak like you're telling a friend about something you found.
- ❌ **Don't dunk on competitors by name in a hostile way.** Light dunks are fine ("HandBrake's UI is from 2007"). Aggressive dunks read as desperate.
- ❌ **Don't show fake screen recordings.** If you say "converts in 8 minutes," actually time it on camera.
- ❌ **Don't add "subscribe to my channel" before the CTA.** Cast's CTA comes first; your channel CTA comes after.

## 8. The visual style

Cast's brand is **clean, greyscale, modern**. Your video should feel adjacent.

- **Color grade:** clean, slightly desaturated. Avoid warm orange-and-teal LUTs.
- **Music:** soft electronic, lo-fi piano, or no music. NO copyright-strike trap music.
- **B-roll:** clean desk, single monitor, no clutter. The product should be the prettiest thing on screen.
- **Text overlays:** sans-serif, white or black, never colored. Match the website.
- **Pacing:** quick but not frantic. Cuts every 1.5–3 seconds.

If you want a reference: look at **MKBHD's short-form**, **Casey Neistat's product reveals**, **the early days of Levelsio's launch videos**. NOT Mr. Beast pacing. NOT YouTube-thumbnail face.

Truth in advertising matters. Everything below is verifiable.

- **Buy once, $19** — actually $19, actually one-time, actually no subscription
- **macOS, Windows, Linux** — one purchase covers all three
- **Converts MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI, GIF, MP3, M4A, WAV** — and 25+ input formats
- **Parallel conversion across CPU cores** — up to your full core count
- **Drag-and-drop folders** — recursively
- **Never overwrites your source file** — auto-numbers collisions
- **Lifetime updates, free** — every future version included
- **30-day money-back guarantee** — no questions
- **Open source under GPL-3.0** — you can read the code on GitHub
- **No telemetry, no accounts, no phone-home** — yank the ethernet, still works

### Things you should NOT claim

- ❌ "The fastest converter in the world" (we haven't benchmarked)
- ❌ "Used by [famous company]" (not yet)
- ❌ "Award-winning" (no awards)
- ❌ "Trusted by millions" (we just launched)
- ❌ Any specific user count, sales number, or testimonial you didn't get from us

## 12. The "make me look smart" cheat sheet

If a creator wants to sound technically credible in their video, here's the depth-without-jargon language:

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| "It uses FFmpeg" | "Under the hood, it's the same engine the pros use" |
| "Parallel multiprocessing" | "Converts a bunch at the same time, not one after the other" |
| "Hardware-accelerated encoding" | (we don't have this yet — don't claim it) |
| "Cross-platform PySide6 application" | "Works on Mac, Windows, and Linux — same app, same buttons" |
| "Lossy compression with CRF 23" | "Files come out small enough to share but still look great" |

## 14. When you're stuck

If you can't think of a hook, default to **show the drop**. A 5-second clip of dropping a folder of files onto Cast and watching the queue light up sells the product better than any voiceover.

If you can't think of a CTA, default to **"$19 once, link in bio."**

If you can't think of a pain point, default to **"I used to use HandBrake."** Half your audience knows exactly what that means.
